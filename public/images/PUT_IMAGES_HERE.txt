Optional — real photos go here (e.g. temple.jpg, couple photos for the gallery).

To swap a colored placeholder for a real photo:

1.  Copy the image into this folder:  public/images/temple.jpg
2.  In the component, replace:

        <Placeholder label="..." className="..." />

    with:

        import Image from "next/image";
        <Image src="/images/temple.jpg" alt="Temple" fill className="object-cover" />

    Keep the surrounding positioned parent div + className so the layout
    and GSAP animation refs stay untouched.
